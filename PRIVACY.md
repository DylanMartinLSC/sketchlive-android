# SketchLive privacy policy

_Effective 27 October 2026. Applies to the SketchLive Android app (`dev.sketchlive.android`)._

SketchLive generates images entirely on your phone. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## What stays on your phone

- **Sketches, descriptions (prompts), settings, and generated images** are stored only in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Content reports** you file with **Report** are saved in the app's private storage. SketchLive uses them to remove the reported image and to block that description on your phone.
- Images you **Save** go to `Pictures/SketchLive` in your gallery. Images you **Share** go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet.

## Network use

The app uses the internet only to download the AI model files you choose. They come from Hugging Face (`huggingface.co`) and GitHub (`github.com`). Those requests contain no sketches, prompts, images, or identifiers created by SketchLive. As with any download, those hosts receive standard connection data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

## Reports you choose to send

If you tick **Also email the details to the developer** when reporting, SketchLive opens your email app with a prefilled message. It contains the report reason, prompt, style, mode, seed, and app version, but no image. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. Emailed reports are used only to improve content safeguards and are deleted within 12 months.

## Children

SketchLive is not directed at children under 13.
