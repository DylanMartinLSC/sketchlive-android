SketchLive Android SDXS Sketch model pack v1

This is a format conversion of existing models, not newly trained weights.
Changes: static 256 px ONNX graphs, FP16 weights, FP32 float inputs/outputs,
and a fused SDXS UNet + Sketch ControlNet graph. No training or fine-tuning.

Base: IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper
Revision: 76f720262bb051da75666b22c902a78c8e16c763
https://huggingface.co/IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper

ControlNet: IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper-sketch
Revision: cd341678087336ed72c05660ac84d765896b48e7
https://huggingface.co/IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper-sketch

SDXS authors: Yuda Song, Zehao Sun, Xuanwu Yin.

https://github.com/DylanMartinLSC/sketchlive-android

Pipeline: tokenize with SD1 CLIP (PAD=49407), encode the prompt, build a white
sketch-on-black control map, sample fixed-seed Gaussian noise at t999,
perform one Euler denoising step with Sketch ControlNet, decode with TAESD.
Colored/filled sketches use Sobel edges; monochrome ink on white is inverted.
Fast mode does not support multiple denoising steps; use Quality for Enhance.

Download all files in manifest.json beside their .data weight files.
All sizes and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the manifest.
Actual phone speed and memory use require hardware testing.
